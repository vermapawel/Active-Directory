Kerberos is an authentication protocol that uses a trusted third party to facilitate secure authentication between a client and a server. During this process, two types of tickets are used:

Ticket Granting Ticket (TGT): Used to authenticate a user to the Kerberos realm and obtain access to additional services.

Ticket Granting Service (TGS) Ticket, also known as a Service Ticket (ST): Used to authenticate the user to a specific service or application.

Forged Ticket Attacks: In these attacks, an attacker extracts sensitive information from the domain, such as Kerberos keys or account credentials, and uses this information to create malicious or forged Kerberos tickets. These forged tickets can then be used to gain unauthorized access to resources and services within the environment.

There are different types of Forging attacks

**Golden Ticket Attacks**

This attack allows to create forged TGT without interacting with KDC. 

The initial exchange of AS-REP and AS-REQ messages are skipped.

<img width="661" height="212" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dea2f7c0-f687-4ad5-8bf4-ac207bb1de22" />

The TGT is the 1st ticket generated by the KDC. 

In AS-REP there is TGT which is encrypted by KDC key. Later we use TGT to obtain a Service Ticket (ST)

So, by forging a malicious TGT, which is called a Golden Ticket, we are skipping the 1st step and continue with 3rd message i.e TGS-REQ.

<img width="715" height="243" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/57d52c77-9816-4c1b-b2f0-bee2084ad7d0" />

In a Golden Ticket attack, an attacker who has obtained the KRBTGT account hash can forge a malicious TGT offline without communicating with the KDC.

Because the forged TGT appears legitimate, the attacker can effectively bypass the initial AS-REQ / AS-REP authentication phase and directly proceed with the TGS-REQ step to request service tickets for any service in the domain.

As a result, the attacker can impersonate any domain user, including highly privileged accounts such as Domain Administrators, and gain unauthorized access to domain resources.

Requirements for a Golden Ticket Attack

-Domain Name

-Domain SID

-KRBTGT account NTLM hash (or Kerberos key)

-Username and group information to impersonate
